Torque budget

Rated 18 Nm continuous. Lobe contact stress reaches PA-CF yield at about 30 Nm, so keep peaks under ~24 Nm.

A NEMA 17 at 0.45 Nm delivers roughly 19 Nm through this at 85 % efficiency, so the motor is effectively the fuse. The way to overload it is to back-drive the arm into something — that path bypasses the motor entirely.

Orientation note: with the arm horizontal, gravity loads the same side of the ring continuously and that side wears first. If the arm has a home position, vary it. The pin circle is symmetric, so rotating the front plate 180° later gives you a fresh side.

If something is wrong

diagnosis
SymptomMost likely causeWhat to do
Periodic tight spot, once per input revolution Cam runout, or a 6802 pressed on crooked Strip to step 3 and re-run the indexing check. Reprint the shaft in PC if runout is off.
Tight spot at a fixed housing position A leaning or bent ring pin Straightedge across the pin ends to find it. Replace the pin; reprint the cup if the hole has gone loose.
Uniformly stiff, warms quickly at no load Profile clearance too small, or the printed pin circle is undersize Measure first, then raise PROFILE_CLR by 0.03 at a time and reprint the discs.
Backlash grows over hours of use Lobe flanks creeping — running above ~24 Nm, or dry Re-grease, cap peak torque. If it persists, the fix is machined POM or nylon discs, not thicker printed ones.
Clunk on load reversal Bedded-in flanks plus the normal 0.10 mm profile clearance Expected at some level. If it grows, treat as the row above.
It skips under load Almost certainly a loose front-plate pin seat The model says skip needs >300 Nm with both ends supported. If it skips at working torque, a pin is not held at both ends. Check the front plate holes against the coupon.
Arm droops a fraction of a degree and stays there Printed flange body creeping under the constant moment Reprint the flange in PA-CF at higher infill, or shorten the reach. Bearings are not the issue — the load is 2.6 % of the 6810's rating.
Notchy, gritty rotation from new A bearing brinelled during pressing Find it by feel and replace it. Always press on the race you are fitting, never through the balls.
