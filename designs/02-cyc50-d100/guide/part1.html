Before you touch a part

gate 0

Two things decide whether this build works, and both happen before assembly: the press fits, and the disc material. Get them wrong and no amount of careful assembly saves it.

Print check

Coupon calibration

Print coupon_pins_inserts.stl in the cup / front-plate material and profile, and coupon_bearings.stl in the disc / flange material. Same nozzle, same speeds, same layer height as the real parts.

Do not skip this

A loose front-plate pin hole turns a pin supported at both ends into a cantilever. That single change is most of the difference between this design and the one that skips.

Tools

Arbor press or a vice with soft jaws · 2.5 mm hex key (ball end helps) · soldering iron with an insert tip, 220–240 °C · dial indicator + stand or V-blocks · calipers · small square · PTFE or lithium grease · deburring tool.
